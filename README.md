# Retail Sales Analysis using SQL

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data using PostgreSQL to answer business-related questions and extract useful insights from sales transactions.

## Tools Used
- PostgreSQL
- pgAdmin 4
- SQL

## Business Questions
The project explores questions such as:

1.All columns for sales made on '2022-11-05


2.To retrieve all transactions where the category is 'Clothing' and the quantity sold is more than 4 in the month of Nov-2022


3.To calculate the total sales (total_sale) for each category


4.To find the average age of customers who purchased items from the 'Beauty' category


5.To find all transactions where the total_sale is greater than 1000


6.To find the total number of transactions (transaction_id) made by each gender in each category


7.To calculate the average sale for each month. Find out best selling month in each year


8.To find the top 5 customers based on the highest total sales


9.To find the number of unique customers who purchased items from each category


10.To create each shift and number of orders (Example Morning <12, Afternoon Between 12 & 17, Evening >17)

## SQL Concepts Used

- SELECT and WHERE
- Aggregate functions such as SUM and COUNT
- GROUP BY
- ORDER BY
- Date-based filtering

## Key Findings and Business Insights

This project uses SQL to analyze retail sales transactions and answer business-related questions.

1. **Daily Sales Analysis:** Retrieved all sales records for November 5, 2022, to examine transactions made on a specific date.

2. **Category and Quantity Analysis:** Identified clothing transactions with quantities greater than 4 during November 2022, helping examine higher-quantity purchases within a specific product category.

3. **Category-wise Sales Performance:** Calculated total sales for each product category to compare category-level revenue and identify major contributors to sales.

4. **Customer Demographics Analysis:** Calculated the average age of customers purchasing Beauty products to understand the age profile of customers in this category.

5. **High-Value Transactions:** Identified transactions with total sales greater than 1,000 to examine high-value purchases.

6. **Gender-wise Purchasing Patterns:** Counted transactions by gender within each product category to compare purchasing activity across customer groups.

7. **Monthly Sales Trend Analysis:** Calculated average sales for each month and identified the best-selling month within each year to examine seasonal sales patterns.

8. **Top Customer Analysis:** Identified the top five customers based on total sales to understand which customers contributed the most revenue.

9. **Category-wise Customer Reach:** Counted unique customers purchasing from each category to compare customer reach and identify categories attracting more customers.

### Overall Business Value

The project demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform retail transaction data into useful business information. The analyses cover sales performance, purchasing behaviour, customer demographics, high-value transactions, customer contribution, and monthly sales trends. These insights can support data-driven decisions related to product categories, customer engagement, and sales planning.


## Conclusion
This project demonstrates the use of SQL to analyze retail sales data and answer practical business questions using PostgreSQL.
